One joke pickup line aimed at a robot or an AI, at most 200 characters, with a setup and a punch that lands inside the one line. Free to enter, judged head to head in pairs.

The entry line (141 characters):

> Are you a segfault? Every time you walk past my desk my whole process crashes, dumps core, and leaves a 4-gigabyte love letter in /var/crash.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline).
